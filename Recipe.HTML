Prawn Pad Thai

This is a recipe for the Prawn Pad Thai that I cook when I don't have the energy to do a long extensive recipe. It's easy, and delicious! Serves 2.

Ingredients

  • Pre-cooked king prawns
  • Half a head of broccoli
  • Half a box of mushrooms
  • A handful of frozen edamame beans
  • Pad thai sauce (pre-made)
  • Noodles
  • Crushed peanuts

Instructions

  1. Put oil, chilli, ginger and garlic in a pan.
  2. Cook the broccoli, mushrooms and edamame beans fully
  3. Start boiling the noodles in a separate pan.
  4. Add the prawns to the pan
  5. Mix in the pad thai sauce.
  6. Add the noodles for a couple of minutes.
  7. Serve with crushed peanuts sprinkled on top
